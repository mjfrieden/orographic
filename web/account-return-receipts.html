Local account return receipts

This preview reads a file you select into memory in the current browser tab. It does not upload or retain it, fetch broker history, or verify the authenticity of the supplied observations. Account and environment must match the loaded broker account. Clearing, changing account/session, leaving the page or reloading removes the preview.

Required evidence

Supply total account net-liquidation valuations, including cash and open holdings, at the start and end of the period and immediately before and after every external funding event. The before/after values must reconcile to the signed flow within one cent. Include deposits and withdrawals, including valued in-kind transfers; do not classify dividends, interest, trading proceeds, commissions or fees as external funding. Each interval must have complete funding coverage. If that cannot be established, do not set the coverage declarations to true.

All valuations must be between $0.01 and $1 trillion. Growth indices outside 0.000000000001–1,000,000,000,000,000 are rejected to keep arithmetic within supported bounds. Accounts crossing zero, leverage losses below zero, missing valuations, ambiguous funding timing, foreign-currency conversion, and incomplete history are unsupported. No annualization, benchmark, calendar-period interpolation or investment attribution is inferred. Source provenance is a user assertion, not verification.

JSON format

The following is a synthetic format example only; it is never loaded by default. Replace every account, source, time and amount with supported observations before use. Use canonical UTC timestamps including milliseconds. Supply 2–1,000 observations in strictly increasing time order, no future timestamps, and a file no larger than 1 MiB.

Calculation

The opening observation must have zero flow; begin immediately after opening funding. For each later observation, subperiod return is its pre-flow equity divided by the previous observation’s post-flow equity, minus one. The period return compounds those growth factors. The example produces 21%, $500 net funding, and $260 cash-flow-adjusted dollar change. An external deposit alone produces no investment return.

The equity line shows supplied post-flow account values and includes funding. The separate growth index starts at 100 and excludes external funding effects. Lines connect observations only and do not establish intraperiod peaks or drawdowns. The table supplies exact observations and subperiod returns. The observed date range is explicit; the result is not a current broker valuation.

Methodology reference: GIPS Standards Handbook for Firms describes valuation at external flows and geometric linking. This preview does not claim GIPS compliance.

What is still missing for broker-backed performance?

Orographic currently has a balance snapshot and bounded closed-position history. Automated broker-backed account returns require a separately authorized, provenance-preserving collection of dated net-liquidation valuations and complete deposits/withdrawals, with before/after valuations at every flow, identity separation, durable retention and reconciliation. Realized trade gains cannot substitute for those observations.
